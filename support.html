Your OS Support

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Your OS helps you understand daily energy, water balance, and readiness through one YOS Score and one clear action at a time.

Getting started

  • Complete the short intro, then open Today.
  • Tap Done on the main action card to log water balance and update your score.
  • Use Why? to see how today’s recommendation was chosen.

Subscriptions

Your OS Pro unlocks full prediction, weekly patterns, Your Book, and related Pro capabilities. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple.

  • Restore: Settings → What’s in Pro → Restore purchases, or open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or cancel: Settings → What’s in Pro → Manage Subscription, or iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Language & appearance

Settings → Language (tap Apply after choosing). Settings → Appearance for light, dark, or system theme.

Data & privacy

Your events and score history stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
