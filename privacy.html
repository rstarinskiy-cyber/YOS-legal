Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Overview

Your OS (“we”, “our”, “the app”) helps you understand your daily energy, water balance, and readiness through a single YOS Score. We designed the app to keep your data on your device.

Data we collect

Stored on your device: life events you log (e.g. water balance, activity), YOS Score history, and app settings (language, appearance). This data stays on your device unless you share it via the system share sheet.

We do not collect (MVP): account registration, email, third-party analytics SDKs, or sale of personal data by default.

Health data

Your OS does not require Apple Health for core functionality. With your permission, Your OS may read optional Apple Health data — such as sleep analysis, step count, and related activity signals — to refine on-device readiness estimates. Health data stays on your device and is governed by Apple’s Health permissions. Your OS does not write data to Apple Health.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive payment card details. Apple may provide subscription status to unlock Pro features.

Notifications

If enabled, local reminders are scheduled from on-device YOS Score state. Notification content is generated on your device.

Children’s privacy

Your OS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data retention

Data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset app data.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Deleting the app removes locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy and post the revised version at this URL with an updated effective date.
